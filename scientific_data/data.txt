SEM: Scanning Electron Microscope. Used for high resolution imaging of solid samples. Requires dry and clean surface.
XRD: X-Ray Diffraction. Used for crystal structure analysis. Sample must be powdered for best results.
FTIR: Fourier Transform Infrared Spectroscopy. Used for chemical bond analysis. Sample can be liquid, solid, or gas.
TEM: Transmission Electron Microscope. High-magnification imaging of thin sample slices. Requires ultra-thin sections.
NMR: Nuclear Magnetic Resonance. Determines physical and chemical properties of atoms. Commonly used for organic molecules in liquid state.
DSC: Differential Scanning Calorimetry. Measures heat flow associated with phase transitions. Sample must be solid or liquid in a crimped pan.
TGA: Thermogravimetric Analysis. Measures weight change during heating. Used for thermal stability and composition.
XPS: X-ray Photoelectron Spectroscopy. Surface chemistry analysis. Requires high vacuum and dry samples.
ICP-OES: Inductively Coupled Plasma Optical Emission Spectroscopy. Trace metal analysis in liquid samples.
HPLC: High-Performance Liquid Chromatography. Separates components in a liquid mixture. Requires liquid phase samples.
GC-MS: Gas Chromatography-Mass Spectrometry. Identifies volatile substances in a gas or liquid mixture.
RAMAN: Raman Spectroscopy. Vibrational mode analysis. Non-destructive, works for most sample types.
AFM: Atomic Force Microscope. Nano-scale surface profiling. Works on solids in air or liquid.
UV-Vis: Ultraviolet-Visible Spectroscopy. Measures light absorption. Usually for liquid samples in a cuvette.
EDX: Energy Dispersive X-ray Spectroscopy. Elemental analysis often paired with SEM.
PL: Photoluminescence. Analyzes electronic structure of semiconductors.
BET: Brunauer-Emmett-Teller Analysis. Measures surface area and porosity of solids. Requires dry powders.
DLS: Dynamic Light Scattering. Measures particle size in liquid suspension.
MOKE: Magneto-Optic Kerr Effect. Analyzes magnetic properties of thin films.
STM: Scanning Tunneling Microscope. Atomic-scale imaging of conductive surfaces.
SQUID: Superconducting Quantum Interference Device. Measures extremely subtle magnetic fields.
EPR: Electron Paramagnetic Resonance. Studies species with unpaired electrons.
VSM: Vibrating Sample Magnetometer. Measures magnetic moment of solid or liquid samples.
AES: Auger Electron Spectroscopy. High-resolution surface elemental analysis.
XRF: X-ray Fluorescence. Elemental composition of solids and liquids.
SAXS: Small-Angle X-ray Scattering. Analyzes nano-structures (1-100 nm).
Nanoindenter: Measures mechanical properties (hardness, modulus) at the nano-scale.
Ellipsometer: Measures thickness and optical constants of thin films.
Zeta Potential: Measures surface charge of particles in liquid.
Fluorescence Microscope: Visualizes biological samples using fluorescent tags.
Confocal Microscope: High-resolution 3D imaging of biological or material samples.
